# YYYY-MM-DD

**Index:** three to six keyword lines summarising the day. This block is the
search accelerator — future sessions read it before anything else, so write it
for someone who was not there.

- What actually got done, with the detail that makes it verifiable later.
  Link to the area note that holds the knowledge: [[Some Area Note]]

## Decisions

- What was decided, **and why**. The reasoning is the part that ages well.
  Record decisions you disagreed with too, marked as such, without editorialising.

## Open

- What is unfinished, blocked, or waiting on someone else.
