## The rules that matter most

1. **Chronicle, not encyclopedia.** The daily note points at knowledge; the
   knowledge lives in the area notes.
2. **Write back immediately.** Not at the end of the session.
3. **Consolidate.** Extend an existing note before creating a new one.
4. **Every fact is timeless, dated, or a pointer.**
